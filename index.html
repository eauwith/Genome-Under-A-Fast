Scale — nucleus · ~10 µm

3D genome architecture

Inside the nucleus, chromosomes occupy distinct territories. Within a territory, a gene's neighborhood can sit in the A compartment (open, transcriptionally active, nuclear interior) or the B compartment (closed, near the nuclear lamina). Locally, CTCF/cohesin extrude a loop that brings an enhancer into contact with the gene's promoter.

Scale — cell · ~20 µm

The cell and its organelles

Zooming out from the nucleus to the whole cell. Fasting is not only a nuclear event — it visibly remodels the cytoplasm. Watch the autophagosomes appear and multiply, mitochondria increase and elongate, lipid droplets shrink as they're burned for fuel, and ribosomes thin out on the rough ER as mTOR-driven protein synthesis is dialled down.

Scale — chromatin fiber · ~10 nm

Nucleosome packing & histone marks

Each bead is a nucleosome. Wide, even spacing reads as open, accessible euchromatin; tight, overlapping spacing reads as condensed heterochromatin. Cones above the promoter-proximal nucleosomes show which post-translational modifications (PTMs) dominate.

Scale — DNA · single base pairs

DNA methylation landscape

Large pink beads on the helix are methylated CpGs; the small plain beads are unmethylated. CpG islands at promoters stay almost fully unmethylated no matter the state — they're protected. The action is at the CpG shores flanking them, and — in the opposite direction — the gene body, where methylation tends to track transcription upward.

About this model — what's illustrative vs. established

What's grounded in known biology

The gene set and its direction of response draw on the SIRT1–AMPK–FOXO3–PGC-1α fasting axis (Cantó & Auwerx 2009; Rodgers et al. 2005), mTOR/S6K1 suppression under nutrient restriction, and reduced NF-κB/IL-6 inflammatory tone with fasting. The methylation architecture — CpG islands staying stably unmethylated while flanking CpG shores carry most of the tissue- and state-specific variation — follows Irizarry et al. 2009. Gene-body methylation rising with transcription, opposite the shore/promoter direction, is a well-replicated genomic pattern. TAD/loop structure via CTCF–cohesin and A/B nuclear compartments follow Dixon et al. 2012 and Lieberman-Aiden et al. 2009.

What's simplified for this visualization

Exact percentages, the smooth 0–100 slider, and any single gene's full multiscale trajectory are an illustrative synthesis, not measurements from one dataset. Real fasting epigenomics varies by tissue, sex, species, fasting duration/protocol, and individual metabolic state; much of the direct evidence is from animal models and short human trials rather than large longitudinal human cohorts. SIRT1's main fasting effect is deacetylase activity (via rising NAD⁺), which this model folds into an "expression" bar alongside genuine transcriptional change, for comparability with the other five genes. Nuclear and chromatin geometry (blob shapes, exact spacing, helix pitch) are stylized for legibility, not to scale or structurally literal.
